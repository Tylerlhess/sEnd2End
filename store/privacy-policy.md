# Privacy policy — sEnd2End

Canonical public page: https://tylerlhess.github.io/sEnd2End/privacy.html

Source: [`privacy.html`](../privacy.html) at the repository root.

**Last updated:** 2026-08-24

sEnd2End does not collect your data and does not share it. Keys and settings stay in local browser storage. Connected sites you allow can request encrypt/decrypt; they never receive your private key.
