sEnd2End
On-device end-to-end encryption
Keys never leave this device

This extension holds your RSA private key on this device. Connected sites store only ciphertext and data keys wrapped for people you choose — files, messages, or anything else the site encrypts.

Your key
none

SHA-256 fingerprint of your public key. Share it to let others verify they encrypted to the right person.

Backup

A passphrase-protected backup is the only way to recover this key on another browser or machine. Store it offline.

Connected sites

These origins can ask the plugin to encrypt and decrypt with your key. Add one from the toolbar popup on that tab, or with the site’s Connect sEnd2End button.

Who can open data

On a connected site you pick recipients by role. The page lists each member’s fingerprint, then wraps the data key to those keys only.

  • Uploader / sender — always keeps a wrap for data they encrypt.
  • External auditor — invited reviewers who must open proof.
  • Staff — owners or operators who must open the data.
  • Platform admin — only if an admin must open it. Leave off so operators cannot read it.
Without this extension

Some sites can still generate a key and encrypt in the page with WebCrypto. That is a fallback: a changed site build could see plaintext after decrypt. Keep sEnd2End installed, connect the site, and hold a passphrase backup.
